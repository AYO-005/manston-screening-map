Manston screening map – Group 2 – final, 18 November 2026

We screened the whole 15 km circle around the Manston Met Office station by exclusion: every constraint in Table 3 is removed with its buffer on a 50 m grid, and patches under 0.25 km² are dropped. Four candidate sites remain: onshore clusters A, B and C (zone Z1) and the offshore strip Z2 (click a site for its summary). We chose cluster B and compare three farms of about 35 MW on it, in the same colours as the report: Large Medium Small. Inputs are approximate, so areas are upper bounds and buffers are screening assumptions, not statutory distances.

Candidate sites after screening (click a row or a site on the map)

Areas are measured from the zone outlines on this map (A 10.74, B 3.04, C 1.74, Z1 15.51, Z2 39.12 km²), so Z1 = 15.5 km² (10.74 + 3.04 + 1.74); shown to one decimal, 10.7 + 3.0 + 1.7 = 15.4 by rounding. Width is the extent across the 235° wind; distance is from the nearest zone edge to the Richborough 400 kV substation. The tip-height limit is the airport outer horizontal surface, 150 m above the 54 m aerodrome (about 204 m AOD), minus ground level.

The three farms on cluster B (chosen site)

Each farm uses staggered rows facing exactly 235° (rows run NW–SE across the prevailing SW wind), 5 D within a row and 4 D between rows, fitted to the cluster B outline. Where more positions fit than the farm needs, the positions kept are those with the largest clearance margin. Checks for every turbine: base inside cluster B; nearest road and railway beyond the topple distance (tip height + 10 %, PPG para. 016); 400 kV line beyond 3 D; outside the 800 m dwelling buffer; Roman Fort beyond 500 m. Only A-roads are drawn, so minor roads still need checking at site level. Cable routes are indicative straight runs to the Richborough 400 kV substation. Energy: Large and Medium are the report values (Table 6); the Small farm was re-run on 18 November 2026 with the Nordex N131/3600 on the same Jensen model (Ct 0.8, kw 0.075, hourly 2007 series, z0 0.05 m, losses × 0.932), which reproduces the Large farm exactly (128.1 GWh/yr, 3.2 % wake loss). Hub heights are the tallest published option under the 197.5 m tip limit: SG 7.0-170 110.5 m (Siemens Gamesa, 2026), V150-4.2 105 m (Vestas, n.d.), N131/3600 120 m (Nordex, 2018, tower TS120).
