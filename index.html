SEF01 Wind Energy · Manston site survey · Part A

Where a wind farm can be built within 15 km of Manston

Every constraint and its buffer is removed from the 15 km circle (324 km² of land, 383 km² of sea) on a 50 m grid, and patches under 0.25 km² are dropped. What remains is zone Z1 on the Ash Level and the Little Stour valley west of Sandwich (15.5 km² in three clusters, A, B and C) and the offshore strip Z2 (39 km²) beyond the 2 km coastal exclusion and outside the inshore shipping corridor. The four report options are drawn under Report options: each shows turbine positions that fit inside its zone at the lecture 8 spacing. Every symbol on the map is explained in the layer list; a ring, a line or a label can be hidden with its checkbox. The screening is a first pass with approximate inputs, so every area is an upper bound. Tick layers on and off to see which constraint removes which land.

Fit check: turbine bases are placed on a grid whose rows face the 235° wind, 5 D apart within a row and 4 D between staggered rows onshore (lecture 8), 7 D × 5 D offshore. Every base must lie inside the buildable area, because each buffer is measured from the turbine, and is also checked directly against the drawn features: roads and railways 1.1 × tip height, the 400 kV line 3 D, villages 800 m, the Roman Fort 500 m. Tips stay under the 204 m AOD airport outer horizontal surface. Energy yield and costs are in the Part A and Part B workbooks. Land beside the Richborough substation is not buildable: it lies inside the airport conical surface and under the road, 400 kV, home and nature-site buffers.
